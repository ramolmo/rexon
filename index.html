REXON Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Status: Active

REXON ("Platform", "we", "our") is dedicated to safeguarding workforce and corporate operational data. This policy details our commitments regarding data collection, processing, and isolation.

1. Data Ingestion & Use

We process telephone numbers, demographic data, and valuation records solely for verification, deduplication, and workflow calibration as directed by the authenticated enterprise client.

2. Zero Data Leakage Guarantee

Customer datasets are never aggregated across unassociated tenants. Data submitted by client organizations remains compartmentalized through tokenized database structures.

3. Storage & Encryption

All data at rest is stored in encrypted Postgres tables and Turso Smart Vaults on AWS infrastructure. Transit is secured via TLS 1.3 encryption.

Security Architecture Overview

Detailed architecture briefing on REXON's multi-layered defense model:

  • Identity Gate: Supabase GoTrue authentication with cryptographic session cookies and JWT bearer tokens.
  • Dual Storage Engine: Supabase Postgres + Turso libSQL Tokenized Cloud Vault for zero-data-loss redundancy.
