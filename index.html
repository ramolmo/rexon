Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common operational, technical, and governance questions.

What are the exact age requirements for submitted data? 🛡️

For Female Reports, the individual must be 43 years or older. For Male Reports, the individual must be 41 years or older. Records indicating deceased/unknown ages or empty age fields are accepted, but numeric values below the thresholds are automatically purged by the system.

How does the 420 daily target calculation work? 🎯

Each Gender Verify worker has a daily target of 420 total verified records, divided evenly as 210 Female (43+) and 210 Male (41+). Both progress bars update in real time, and surpassing the target automatically logs performance bonuses into the payroll engine.

What is the 6-hour stock dispenser lock? ⏱️

When a worker claims work numbers from company stock, those records are assigned exclusively to that worker and locked so no other team member receives the same numbers. General workers can claim up to 1,000 numbers every 6 hours, while verified Team Leaders have unlimited access.

How does the Lookup Report Qualifier decide what to keep? 🔍

Rule 1: Household Income $75,000+ is kept even if market value is missing. Rule 2: Market value $350,000+ ($400k+) is kept regardless of income level. Rule 3: Market value > $1.8M is tagged as Special VIP. Records missing dollar signs ($) or failing both conditions are filtered into No Information / Mistake lists for review.

How do I contact technical support or get my account credentials? ✈️

Public registrations are disabled to preserve operational security. Accounts are created directly by administrators. For questions, inquiries, or credential resets, contact leadership directly on Telegram: @rexwarr.

REXON Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Status: Active

REXON ("Platform", "we", "our") is dedicated to safeguarding workforce and corporate operational data. This policy details our commitments regarding data collection, processing, and isolation.

1. Data Ingestion & Use

We process telephone numbers, demographic data, and valuation records solely for verification, deduplication, and workflow calibration as directed by the authenticated enterprise client.

2. Zero Data Leakage Guarantee

Customer datasets are never aggregated across unassociated tenants. Data submitted by client organizations remains compartmentalized through tokenized database structures.

3. Storage & Encryption

All data at rest is stored in encrypted Postgres tables and Turso Smart Vaults on AWS infrastructure. Transit is secured via TLS 1.3 encryption.

Security Architecture Overview

Detailed architecture briefing on REXON's multi-layered defense model:

  • Identity Gate: Supabase GoTrue authentication with cryptographic session cookies and JWT bearer tokens.
  • Dual Storage Engine: Supabase Postgres + Turso libSQL Tokenized Cloud Vault for zero-data-loss redundancy.
  • Automated Deduplication: Real-time hashing prevents duplicate records across files and historical client deliveries.
  • 6-Hour Stock Lock: Concurrency locks prevent simultaneous number claims across distributed workforce nodes.
